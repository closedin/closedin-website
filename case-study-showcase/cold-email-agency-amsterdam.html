Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

What does a cold email agency in Amsterdam actually do?
A cold email agency builds and runs outbound sales campaigns on your behalf. closed:in sets up sending domains and inboxes, builds targeted lead lists with Clay, writes copy that matches the Dutch and European market, runs multi-step sequences through Instantly and wires replies straight into your CRM. The goal is qualified meetings in your calendar, not just emails sent.
Why pick an Amsterdam-based cold email agency over a US one?
European GDPR rules, Dutch and Flemish writing conventions, time zones and local buying behaviour are very different from the US market. An Amsterdam-based agency like closed:in knows the local ICP, writes in the language your prospects actually use, and understands NL/BE B2B sales cycles.
How much does cold email outreach cost in the Netherlands?
Most managed cold email engagements in the Netherlands run between EUR 3K and EUR 8K per month, depending on volume, languages and CRM complexity. closed:in works on a setup fee plus monthly retainer model, with engagements typically running 3 months minimum.
How fast will I see results from cold email campaigns?
Infrastructure setup (domains, inboxes, warmup, list, copy) takes 2 to 3 weeks. Most closed:in clients book their first qualified meeting within 2-3 weeks of launch. Documented results include 78 to 247 opportunities per engagement across NL, BE and the wider European market.
Is cold email still legal under GDPR?
Yes, B2B cold email is allowed under GDPR as long as you have a clear legitimate interest, target business email addresses, identify yourself clearly, and include an opt-out. closed:in builds compliant infrastructure as standard and only targets verified B2B contacts.
Do you also handle LinkedIn outreach, or only cold email?
Both. Cold email and LinkedIn are most effective when combined. We run multi-channel sequences where LinkedIn connection requests, DMs and content amplification reinforce email touches. One operator, one strategy, one CRM.
Which industries do you typically work with?
B2B service companies, SaaS founders, recruitment and PE firms, agencies, e-commerce fulfillment, hospitality SaaS, creative agencies and home interior retail. ACVs typically EUR 5K and up.
