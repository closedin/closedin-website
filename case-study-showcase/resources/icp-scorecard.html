The 12-point ICP scorecard we use on every list

Every account we add to a closed:in client campaign gets scored against 12 signals before it gets touched. We share the scorecard so you can apply it to your own outbound. CSV template free with email signup.

Why 12 signals

Fewer than 8 and you miss obvious mismatches. More than 15 and the scoring system collapses under its own weight. 12 is what works for us across 1,000+ campaigns.

The 4 categories

Firmographic (4 signals)

Company size, industry vertical, geography, funding stage. Standard but essential. We weight company size highest because it correlates most with ACV.

Technographic (3 signals)

Current tech stack, integration compatibility, tech maturity. For SaaS clients this is the highest-signal category. We use Clay + BuiltWith for this.

Intent (3 signals)

Recent hiring patterns, funding events, content engagement, product launches. We use a custom Claygent prompt to detect these across LinkedIn + news + their website.

Fit (2 signals)

Decision-maker reachability (role accessible via email and LinkedIn) and motion-fit (do they buy software via outbound or only via referral).

Scoring

Each signal gets 0-3 points. Total 36 possible. We only campaign accounts scoring 22+ (strong fit). Accounts scoring 15-21 go into a nurture bucket. Below 15: removed.
