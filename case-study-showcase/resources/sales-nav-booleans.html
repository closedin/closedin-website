40+ Sales Navigator boolean search strings

LinkedIn Sales Navigator boolean strings closed:in uses for finding founders, VP Sales, Heads of RevOps, Sales Engineers, and Operations leads across industries. Paste into Sales Nav's "Title" or "Keywords" filter, then add company-size and geography layers. Four examples below, all 40+ in your inbox.

Founders and CEO targeting

Active founders (excluding former and stealth)

("founder" OR "co-founder" OR "ceo") NOT ("former" OR "ex-" OR "stealth" OR "advisor" OR "board")
Removes former founders and advisory-only roles which Sales Nav defaults often miss.

Sales leaders

VP Sales + CRO (excluding job seekers)

("vp sales" OR "vice president sales" OR "vp of sales" OR "chief revenue officer" OR "cro" OR "head of sales") NOT ("open to work" OR "looking for")
"#open_to_work" badges leak into Sales Nav results. This exclusion cleans them.

RevOps and Sales Engineering

RevOps + Sales Operations decision makers

("revops" OR "revenue operations" OR "sales operations") AND ("head" OR "director" OR "vp" OR "lead" OR "senior manager")
Use for selling tooling: Clay, Apollo, Outreach, Salesloft, RB2B etc.

Operations leaders

COO and operations leadership

("coo" OR "chief operating officer" OR "vp of operations" OR "head of operations") NOT ("former" OR "ex-")
Layer: company size 50-1000. COOs are great budget owners for ops tooling.
