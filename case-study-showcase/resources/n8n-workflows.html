10 n8n workflow templates we'd actually use for B2B outbound

closed:in runs outbound on Clay, Instantly and Close. We don't build in n8n internally, but a lot of our peers do. Below are the 10 templates we'd grab first if we were starting an outbound stack in n8n today, with a short note on how we'd actually use each.

LinkedIn outreach

Personalized LinkedIn connection requests with Apollo, GPT-4, Apify & PhantomBuster

Pulls a prospect list from Apollo, scrapes their LinkedIn via Apify, drafts a tailored connection note with GPT-4, sends via PhantomBuster.

Why we picked it: single best n8n equivalent to what we'd run in Clay + HeyReach. Honest reply rates over generic templates, no PhantomBuster lock-in if you swap it for Unipile.
Get template
Cold email

Cold email icebreaker generator with Apify, GPT-4 & website scraping

Takes a list of company URLs, scrapes the homepage and recent content, and writes a contextual one-line opener for each prospect.

Why we picked it: bridges the gap if you don't have Clay budget. The 1-line opener model is what we use in production, just run on a cheaper stack.
Get template
Competitive intel

Competitive ad research & image generator with Apify, GPT-4o & Facebook Ad Library

Scrapes any competitor's Facebook Ad Library, classifies ads into video/image/text, rewrites copy with GPT-4o and generates fresh creative variants.

Why we picked it: we run this exact pattern manually for clients. Automating it gets you a continuous read on what your competitors are spending money on, weekly.
Get template
Competitive intel

AI Facebook ad spy tool with Apify, OpenAI, Gemini & Google Sheets

Continuously monitors competitor ad accounts, logs new creatives to Sheets, summarises angle and CTA with Gemini for video and OpenAI for text.

Why we picked it: the always-on version of the workflow above. Drop it on a weekly cron and you have a competitor intel feed without paying for AdSpy or Foreplay.
Get template
Sales ops

AI premium proposal generator with OpenAI, Google Slides & PandaDoc

From a discovery call transcript or filled form, generates a branded proposal deck in Google Slides and pushes it through PandaDoc for signature.

Why we picked it: if you write proposals manually, this saves an afternoon per deal. We do it in Qwilr; the structure transfers directly.
Get template
Inbound & ops

Automate customer support & calendar bookings with OpenAI GPT & Google Calendar

A support agent that classifies tickets, drafts replies in your voice, and books a meeting on Google Calendar when the request needs a call.

Why we picked it: useful for any agency or SaaS where inbound replies pile up. The booking handoff is the bit that actually saves time.
Get template
Finance ops

Smart invoice collection system with GPT-4.1, Gmail & Google Sheets

Tracks open invoices in Sheets, drafts polite chase emails in Gmail at the right cadence, and stops chasing when payment lands.

Why we picked it: boring but bankable. If you're an agency owner chasing late invoices, this pays for n8n hosting on its own.
Get template
Prospect research

Research ANYTHING and get a PDF report

An n8n agent that researches a company or topic across the web and ships a structured PDF report with linked sources for about $0.50 per run.

Why we picked it: drop a prospect domain in, get a one-pager back. We do account research manually with Perplexity; this productises it.
Watch + download
Content engine

Ultimate Army of Media Agents

A multi-agent system that handles inbox, calendar, contacts, Drive and creative tasks (image gen, social posting) from one orchestration layer.

Why we picked it: mostly inspirational. If you're a solo founder building a content brand, this is the closest blueprint we've seen to a working "AI ops assistant".
Watch + download
LinkedIn content

Faceless short-form video generator

End-to-end pipeline that takes a topic, generates a script, builds matching visuals, syncs audio and outputs a vertical short ready to post.

Why we picked it: for founders posting volume on LinkedIn / TikTok. Quality is "good enough"; the volume is the unlock.
