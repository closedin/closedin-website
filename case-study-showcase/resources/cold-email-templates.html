12 cold email templates that actually converted

Anonymized from live closed:in client campaigns. Per template: industry, ICP, sequence step, exact copy, and the reply rate + opportunity-to-reply ratio it produced. Three on this page, all 12 free in your inbox after signup.

12 templates · 4-15% reply rates · 6 industries · NL · EU · UK · US

How to read these templates

Each template includes the industry, ICP role and seniority, sequence step, exact subject line and body. The reply rate is the real number from the campaign. OTR (opportunity-to-reply) tells you how many of those replies actually qualified as opportunities. A high reply rate with a low OTR is a red flag, not a win.

Names and identifying details are changed. Numbers and copy structure are real.
